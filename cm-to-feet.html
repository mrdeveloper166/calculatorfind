CM to feet conversion with formulas and chart

Centimeter to feet converter is used to convert centimeter value into feet value and gives you results soon. It is a simple and fast calculator to use.

For using this calculator just put the centimeter value and then click calculate button, it will show you the result in the result area. The CM to feet converter divides your given centimeter value by 30.48 and then gives the result. You can learn the method of calculation also with the help of the centimeter to feet conversion table chart below.

How to convert centimeters to feet or how use it

1 centimeter is equal to 0.0328084 feet:

1 cm = (1/30.48) ft = 0.0328084 ft

The distance d in feet (ft) is equal to the distance d in centimeters (cm) divided by 30.48:

d(ft) = d(cm) / 30.48

Example

Convert 20 centimeters to feet:

d(ft) = 20cm / 30.48 = 0.65617ft
