The calculator centimeter to inches is used to convert the centimeter into inches. To convert centimeters into inches, multiply the given centimeter value by 0.393701 inches. For example, 20 cm to inches is equal to 7.874 inches. (i.e.) 20 x 0.393701 = 7.874.

It will display the method of calculation of your query. For using this calculator just put your centimeter value in the given input and click on calculate. It will show the result in integer or decimal. You can also see the answer in fraction value also.

How to convert centimeters to inches and how to use this calculator.

1 centimeter is equal to 0.3937007874 inches:

1cm = (1/2.54)″ = 0.3937007874″

The distance d in inches (″) is equal to the distance d in centimeters (cm) divided by 2.54:

d(″) = d(cm) / 2.54

Example

Convert 20 cm to inches:

d(″) = 20cm / 2.54 = 7.874″
