How many pounds are in a gram?

As there are 453.59237 grams in a pound, to calculate the number of pounds there are in a gram, you need to divide your grams figure by 453.59237. 1 gram is equal to 0.002205 pounds.

How to convert Grams to Pounds with formulas and examples

1 gram (g) is equal to 0.00220462262185 pounds (lbs).

1 g = 0.00220462262185 lb

The mass m in pounds (lb) is equal to the mass m in grams (g) divided by 453.59237:

m(lb) = m(g) / 453.59237

Example

Convert 5g to pounds:

m(lb) = 5 g / 453.59237 = 0.011023 lb
