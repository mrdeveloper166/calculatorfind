Inches

The inch is a unit of length in the imperial and United States customary systems of measurement.

Meter

The metre or meter is the base unit of length in the International System of Units. The SI unit symbol is m



How to convert inches to meters

1 inch is equal to 0.0254 meters:

1″ = 0.0254m

The distance d in meters (m) is equal to the distance d in inches (″) times 0.0254:

d(m) = d(″) × 0.0254

Example

Convert 20 inches to meters:

d(m) = 20″ × 0.0254 = 0.508m
