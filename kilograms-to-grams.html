Kilogram conversion Calculator and chart

The kilogram is the base unit of mass in the International System of Units, the current metric system, having the unit symbol kg

How to convert Kilograms to Grams & vice versa

1 kilogram (kg) is equal to 1000 grams (g).

1 kg = 1000 g

The mass m in grams (g) is equal to the mass m in kilograms (kg) times 1000:

m(g) = m(kg) × 1000

Example

Convert 5kg to grams:

m(g) = 5 kg × 1000 = 5000 g
