Metric Ton

The metric ton used in most other countries is 1,000 kg, equivalent to 2,204.6 pounds avoirdupois.

convert Kilograms to Tons

1 gram (kg) is equal to 1000000 tons (t).

1 kg = (1/1000) t = 0.001 t

The mass m in tons (t) is equal to the mass m in kilograms (kg) divided by 1000:

m(t) = m(kg) / 1000

Example

Convert 5 kg to tons:

m(t) = 5 kg / 1000 = 0.005 t
