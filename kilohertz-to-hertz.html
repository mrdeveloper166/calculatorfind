Kilohertz to hertz formula

The frequency f in hertz (Hz) is equal to the frequency f in kilohertz (kHz) times 1000:

f(Hz) = f(kHz) × 1000

Example

Convert 3 kilohertz to hertz:

f(Hz) = 3kHz × 1000 = 3000Hz
