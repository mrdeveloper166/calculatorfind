Liter to Gallon and how to use it

A gallon is a volumetric unit of measurement. People have used many different gallons throughout history. There is only one type of gallon still commonly used. The litre or liter (l) is a metric unit of volume. It is equal to 1 cubic decimetre, 1000 cubic centimetres or 0.001 cubic metre . A cubic decimetre or litre occupies a volume of 10 cm × 10 cm × 10 cm and this is how it is equal to one-thousandth of a cubic metre.

For using this calculator Just put the liters value and hit the calculate button fot the result. Gallon = Liter Value multiply by 0.264172
