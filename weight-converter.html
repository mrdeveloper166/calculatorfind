Weight conversion and chart

Units of mass and weight conversion chart are discussed here in metric units of mass and weight and customary units of mass and weight. In math when we use the units of mass and weight conversion chart, we normally use gram to represent as the standard unit of mass and weight, and the other standard units are as follows:


1 milligram = 0.001 gram

1 centigram = 0.01 gram

1 decigram = 0.1 gram

1 kilogram = 1000 grams

1 gram = 1000 milligrams
